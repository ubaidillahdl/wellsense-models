This dataset could be used to predict the hemoglobin (HGB) based on four-wavelength PPG signals.

The dataset consists of 152 subjects, each with signal length in the range of 30 seconds to one minute, and 200 Hz sampling frequency.

The wavelength information of signals in the dataset is listed as follows：
channel 1: 660 nm
channel 2: 730 nm
channel 3: 850 nm
channel 4: 940 nm
